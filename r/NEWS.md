# fortvine 1.0.0

* First release: R bindings to the fortvine Fortran library through its C interface.
* Pair copulas: `bicop_dist()`, `dbicop()`, `hbicop()`, `rbicop()`, `bicop()` (fit or select),
  `logLik()`, `print()` and `summary()` methods.
* Vines: `dvine_structure()`, `vinecop_dist()`, `vinecop()` (Dissmann selection or a fixed
  structure), `dvinecop()`, `rvinecop()`, `rosenblatt()`, `inverse_rosenblatt()`.
* Utilities: `pseudo_obs()` and `fortvine_families()` (the 111 families of the library).
